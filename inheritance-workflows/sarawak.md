# Sarawak — Inheritance Process Workflow

## 1. Overview

Research cutoff and access date: **2026-10-11**. **FACT** means supported by the cited material; it does not certify that no later amendment exists. **INFERENCE** means proposed workflow/design reasoning. **UNKNOWN** means **Not found / needs verification**, with the office named. Older schedules, indexed-only evidence and service targets are identified explicitly.

FACT: This jurisdiction needs a local-law route rather than the PDF’s Peninsular template. Cap. 80 preserves a Probate Officer route. In Kuching its text distinguishes Malay/Dayak estates handled by the District Officer from other estates handled by the appointed officer; the official text notes Amanah Raya Berhad Kuching in that role. Elsewhere the district officer is central. The 2023 Probate and Letters of Administration Rules also require review before deciding territorial filing. Native succession/custom and Muslim rules must be separated from the ordinary non-native non-Muslim distribution branch. [sarawak7] [sarawak8] [sarawak9]

The comparison skeleton is the local PDF’s **Prepare → Route → File → Review/hearing → Grant/order → Transfers**. These are product stages, not statutory percentages or a promise of elapsed time. This file maps all four requested case types; the “small estate” branch overlaps religion/will status rather than forming a fourth religion category.

## 3. Process Flow by Case Type

### Muslim

FACT: Sarawak has its own Syariah courts and civil-procedure ordinances. A Muslim estate needs the appropriate Islamic entitlement determination alongside the representation process. UNKNOWN: the current faraid application pack, fee, court allocation, witness requirements and certificate target; obtain them from JKSS. Do not copy MSWP forms or a Peninsular fee band. [sarawak1] [sarawak2]

1. **Establish the estate and competing rights.** Actor: applicant and prospective representative. Submit: death, family tree at each death, property interests, debts, marriage and any hibah/wasiat/sepencarian evidence. Form: intake inventory; state-specific claim forms if adjudication is needed. Fee: document/search costs; local adjudication fees unknown. Typical duration: document-dependent; no verified statewide median. Output: estate boundary and unresolved-rights list. [N25]
2. **Obtain Islamic entitlement determination when required.** Actor: Jabatan Kehakiman Syariah Sarawak. Submit: application, deaths, heir identities/relationships, asset evidence, residence and witnesses required by the registry. Form: local faraid pack described above; do not reuse another state’s form. Fee: see section 5; unknown items require registry assessment. Typical duration: local target above if applicable; otherwise Not found / needs verification with the receiving office. Output: sealed certificate/order, or directions to resolve missing evidence. [N9]
3. **Secure authority to administer.** Actor: the appropriate Probate Officer under Cap. 80, with court referral where required. Submit: verified estate inventory, any required faraid/sepencarian orders and representative evidence. Form: the relevant small-estate, ARB or civil/local application below. Fee: route-specific; see section 5. Typical duration: route-specific; certificate time is not added as an assumed fixed delay. Output: distribution order or authority naming the person entitled to collect/administer.
4. **Settle obligations and implement distribution.** Actor: authorised representative, creditors, asset holders, land registry and, only where it has a relevant role, Majlis Islam Sarawak / Tabung Baitulmal Sarawak. Submit: sealed authority, asset-specific claim packs, valid debt/expense evidence and tax documents. Form: institution forms and local land instrument; see sections 4–5. Fee: institution/registration/stamping/service charges assessed separately. Typical duration: asset-dependent; Not found / needs verification with the receiving office. Output: receipts, registered titles, protected minor shares and beneficiary acknowledgements. [N10] [N12] [N14] [N18]

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Death and estate inventory] --> B{Rights unresolved?}
  B -->|Yes| C[Resolve hibah wasiat sepencarian or heir issue]
  B -->|No| D[Faraid determination if required]
  C --> D
  D --> E[Appropriate administration or distribution authority]
  E --> F[Debts and expenses settled]
  F --> G[Asset claims and land registration]
  G --> H[Reconcile receipts and close]
```

A faraid certificate establishes entitlement; the receiving institution must confirm whether it also accepts the particular order as release authority. Sabah/Sarawak institution exceptions are addressed in the shared asset protocol. Baitulmal is a conditional claimant/administrator/redemption party, not a mandatory processing counter for every Muslim estate. [N13]

### Amanah Raya branch and overlapping routes

FACT: The national summary-estate guide describes movable-only estates up to RM600,000. ARB also acts as administrator/executor and can obtain authority through other routes; those wider services are not capped at RM600,000. The authority is statutory/representative authority, not a power of attorney from a deceased person. UNKNOWN: confirm the statutory basis and local-asset acceptance with the Sarawak branch before treating this national summary threshold as an independently sufficient local route. [N5] [N6]

Fee: written administration quotation needed; do not reuse 2019 fee bands as current. Typical duration: file completeness dependent; no current local median. Output: accepted engagement and verified service route. [N26] [N31]

### Land transmission after any route

FACT: Land transmission is governed by Sarawak’s Land Code (Cap. 81), with Land and Survey registration, rather than NLC s346. The applicable grant/order and estate inventory must support the asset being registered. UNKNOWN: current transmission form identifier, registration tariff and estate-specific processing target. Native Area Land/NCR interests require title and beneficiary-eligibility screening with Land and Survey and the appropriate native authority. [sarawak3]

INFERENCE: Track (a) registry/checklist selected, (b) representative registration where required, (c) beneficiary vesting/transfer or authorised sale, (d) registration accepted, and (e) updated title/search received. An administrator entered on the register is not necessarily the final beneficiary. Verify stamp classification, encumbrances and any required sale/vesting authority before generating the next instrument.

## 7. Pain Points and Bottlenecks

INFERENCE from the authority/document boundaries above; no measured state delay statistics were found:

- Incorrect office or instrument selection causes rejection; an entitlement certificate and representation authority serve different purposes.
- Successive deaths, absent heirs, incomplete marriages/adoptions and inconsistent IDs create evidence gaps before shares can be finalised.
- An omitted asset, disputed hibah/will, encumbrance, restricted title or minor’s share may need a linked proceeding or supplemental authority.
- A sealed grant can coexist with unreleased bank funds or unregistered land; a grant date alone cannot mean completion.
- Old RM2m pages, historical fee sheets and generic court charters can produce false prices or deadlines.

## 9. App Design Implications

INFERENCE — proposed product rules, not agency requirements:

- Use the [unified status model](README.md#unified-status-model) with separate entitlement, authority and per-asset workstreams. Do not hard-code the PDF’s 15/10/15/20/15/25 weights as legal progress.
- Capture religion at death; domicile/residence; death sequence; citizenship/native status; asset jurisdiction and title category; will/executor; nomination date and capacity; ownership fraction; values with valuation date; debts; heirs alive at each death; guardian/trustee; disputes and prior case numbers.

No absence-of-reform claim is made: failure to locate a later instrument is recorded as an open question. A second independent current source was not found for most local fee items; the sources list makes this limitation reviewable.
